🌻 Garden Guard 🐝

Plant flower-towers, command the bees, and hold 10 waves to save the harvest.

🌱 Plant: pick a flower below (or keys 1 / 2 / 3), then click a grass cell.

🐛 Defend: pests follow the cream path to the gate — each one through costs a life.

🌼 Cross-pollinate: plant different flowers side by side and they grow stronger together. All three together make a Rainbow Bed!

🐝 Swarm: press B to loose the bees when the garden is overrun.

🏆 Win: clear all 10 waves. Lose all 20 lives and the harvest is gone.

Space send wave · B bee swarm · P pause · M mute · R restart
